<objective>
Extend the proven store harness to the other three documents, so all four write paths PERSIST-03 names are under
test: `templateList`, `deviceList` and `formulaList` (pageList is proven in plan 01-01).

Purpose: D-02 requires convergence for **all four** documents, and D-01 asserts each store already applies an
inbound change and suppresses its own echo. That claim is untested today, and the three stores are not
interchangeable: `templates` mirrors `pages` (guard + failure flag), while `registry` and `formulas` have
neither a `connected` guard nor a failure flag, and `formulas` seeds *and writes back* on an unreadable read.
This plan proves what each store actually does — including the gaps — rather than widening behaviour.
Output: `src/lib/stores/templates.svelte.test.ts`, `src/lib/registry.svelte.test.ts`,
`src/lib/formulas.svelte.test.ts` (all new).

are carried verbatim in `must_haves.assumptions` above. The phase-level prohibition recall's 5 items include the
one this plan carries (`must_haves.prohibitions`); the other four live in 01-01 (1), 01-02 (2) and 01-04 (1).
